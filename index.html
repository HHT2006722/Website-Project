Hi, I'm Han Hongtao

I photograph animals. Not wildlife photography — the animals I shoot are in pet markets, indoor exhibits and behind fences. Bad light, fingerprints on the glass, and nothing that cooperates. This site is my notebook.

See the photos Read the notes

All six photos were taken on 5 April 2024 with an iPhone 13 Pro.

What I'm working on

  • Shooting through glass — reflections, water marks and fingerprints that won't wipe off; it is the first problem to solve (Scales, Little Otter)
  • Low light — no flash, so I work with ceiling spots and exposure compensation (Side by Side, Foraging)
  • Waiting for the eyes — an animal looking at the lens decides whether the frame works; the rest is waiting
